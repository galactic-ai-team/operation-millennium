### Authors' Notation Conventions Used Throughout `[Empirical Observation]`
- $G = (V, E)$: finite, undirected, **loopless** graph; **parallel edges are allowed** and counted separately (Section 2.1, p. 7).
- "2-edge-connected": connected, $|V(G)| \ge 2$, and no bridges (Section 1.2, p. 4; Section 2.1, p. 7).
- $\ell(F)$ (written $l(F)$ in the PDF): encoding length of CNF $F$ (Section 1, p. 2; Section 7, p. 20).
- $|F|$: number of clauses in $F$ (Section 1, p. 2).
- $S(F)$ and $S_{\mathrm{reg}}(F)$: minimum unrestricted and regular resolution refutation lengths of $F$, counting **used input clauses and derived clauses** (unused input clauses need not be listed); weakening is allowed in the general definition and eliminated via Proposition 2.9 (Section 1, p. 2; Section 2.3, p. 9).
- $\mathrm{tw}(\emptyset) = -1$ (Section 2.1, p. 7).

## 3. Issues, Gaps & Under-Specified Points Found

Every item below is categorized by severity and tagged with its claim level. **No fatal mathematical flaw was found in the core arguments of Theorems 1.1 or 1.4**, though several small boundary/specification gaps and external-dependency interfaces must be checked or tightened before team-level `Proved` adoption `[Empirical Observation]`.

1. **Satisfiable-component input to Promise Algorithm in Theorem 7.1 (p. 21, lines 7–8) — `MEDIUM` `[Empirical Observation]` / `[Proved]`:**
   - Proposition 2.14 (p. 11) specifies behavior *only* on unsatisfiable CNFs, yet Theorem 7.1 (p. 21) invokes it in parallel on all primal-graph components $F_1, \dots, F_r$, some of which are satisfiable. Requires an explicit soundness clause in Prop. 2.14 (never emits a false refutation or crashes the scheduler on SAT inputs) or a polytime refutation verifier wrapping each parallel worker.
2. **Missing $\|V(G)\| \ge 2$ ($d_v \ge 1$) guard in Section 6 gadget construction (p. 17, line 25) — `LOW` `[Proved]`:**
   - Replacing every vertex $v$ by $X_{d_v}$ invokes Lemma 6.2 ($n \ge 1$), which fails when $G = K_1$ ($d_v = 0$). Theorem 6.1 still holds trivially on $K_1$ ($\mathrm{tw}(L(K_1)) = -1$), and Theorem 7.1 explicitly intercepts $\bot \in F$ on line 1, so the gap is localized to the preamble of Lemma 6.4 / Theorem 6.1.
3. **Small-$b$ ($1 \le b \le 4$) constant handling in Theorem 3.3 (p. 14, lines 12–15) — `LOW` `[Proved]`:**
   - Both Case 1 ($\frac{1}{2}(4/(3+p))^{b/4}$) and Case 2 ($2^{b/2 - 2}$) drop below $1$ for small $b \ge 1$, so concluding $S(G,c) \ge 2^{\Omega_p(b)}$ with a strictly positive exponent constant requires noting $S(G,c) \ge 2$ whenever $b \ge 1$ (via Cor. 2.12).
4. **Multigraph vs. Simple-Graph Transfer across External Citations — `MEDIUM` `[Empirical Observation]`:**
   - TR26-332 allows parallel edges throughout (Section 2.1, p. 7), which is essential because Proposition 4.1 doubles edges! However, several external citations are stated in their original sources (or in TR26-332's own Section 1.1) for simple graphs:
     - **Smoothness remark (Section 1.2, p. 5):** Uses $w(T(G,c)) \le O(\mathrm{tw}(L(G))) + 1$ citing Galesi–Talebanfard–Torán `[16]`, which p. 4 itself restricts to *connected simple graphs*, yet p. 5 applies it to *2-edge-connected graphs* (multigraphs in TR26-332's convention) without checking parallel edges.
     - **Lemmas 5.2 & 5.3 (`[15, Lem. 30]`, `[12, Lem. 12]`, p. 16) and Prop. 2.2 (`[17]`, p. 8):** Must verify in Ace's second-pass audit that all three external statements hold verbatim for loopless multigraphs or add the 1-line reduction to the underlying simple graph.
5. **Bollobás `[7]` citation scope in Lemma 6.2 (p. 17, lines 12–16) — `LOW` `[Empirical Observation]`:**
   - `[7]` handles random $r$-regular graphs of sufficiently large order $n$ ($rn$ even); covering *every* $n \ge 1$ requires an explicit trivial small-$n$ fallback (paths/cycles/$K_1$) and even $r$.
6. **Filtration & Stutter-Step Scope in Lemma 4.4 reduction (p. 15, lines 18–26) — `LOW` `[Proved]`:**
   - The proof of Proposition 4.1 applies Lemma 4.4 while ignoring exchanges outside $B$ and letting move selection depend on tree edges outside $B$. The supermartingale proof in Section 4.1 handles this without change once formulated over the full process filtration.
7. **Encoding-Length ($\ell(F)$) vs. Clause-Count ($|F|$) Hygiene — `INFO` `[Proved]`:**
   - Checked cleanly across Sections 6 and 7: Theorem 6.1 bounds $S(G,c) + \|T(G,c)\|$ (clause count), and Theorem 7.1 uses $\|F\| \le \ell(F)$ (valid whenever $\bot \notin F$, since every nonempty clause requires $\ge 1$ bit/symbol) and $r \le \ell(F)$. Note that in the disconnected case, invoking $\ell(F_i) \le \ell(F)$ assumes variable IDs are not blown up when partitioning $F$ into $F_1, \dots, F_r$ (standard in DIMACS/list encodings).
8. **External Citation Locator & Proof-System Check for `[8]` Broering & Lokam (2003) — `MEDIUM` `[Empirical Observation]`:**
   - No confirmed chapter DOI was found for `[8]` (`locator not found`), and its output regularity/runtime was not independently inspected. Note that Route B in Proposition 2.14 (`[2]` Alekhnovich & Razborov 2011, Theorem 2.3 + $\mathrm{bw}(H(F)) \le \mathrm{tw}(P(F)) + 1$, already audited in `WIDTH_TSEITIN_LITERATURE.md` T11) avoids relying on `[8]` entirely, **provided** the branchwidth-to-primal-treewidth decomposition construction runs deterministically in $\ell^{O(1)} 2^{O(\mathrm{tw}(P(F)))}$ time (using `[6]`)—which should be verified explicitly if Route B is preferred.
